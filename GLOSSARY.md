# Personal Site

A one-person website for writing, photography, showcased works and interactive experiments, written and run entirely by its Owner.

## Language

### People

**Owner**:
The single person who writes, curates and administers the entire site. There are no other authors or editors.
_Avoid_: Admin, author, user

**Studio**:
The Owner's private editor for creating and changing all content, reached at a hidden address behind a login.
_Avoid_: Admin, CMS, control room, dashboard, backend

### Languages

**Translation**:
The Indonesian version of a Post, Work or Readme page. English is always written first; Translations are optional per item.
_Avoid_: Localization, version

**Fallback**:
What a reader sees when they ask for Indonesian and no Translation exists: the English original with a notice.
_Avoid_: Default, missing translation

### Content

**Post**:
A piece of long-form writing published on the site.
_Avoid_: Article, blog, entry, writing

**Series**:
An ordered sequence of Posts meant to be read in order. Each Post belongs to at most one Series.
_Avoid_: Collection, episodes, thread

**Photo**:
A single photograph published in the photography section, with its own descriptive details.
_Avoid_: Picture, image, shot

**Archive**:
The section of the site that holds Photos and saved links, loosely grouped and browsable.
_Avoid_: Gallery, photography, photos page

**Link**:
A saved external web page kept in the Archive, with the Owner's note about why it is worth keeping.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, URL, reference

**Archive item**:
Either a Photo or a Link; the things the Archive is made of.
_Avoid_: Entry, media

**Album**:
An optional, named grouping of Archive items. Items can belong to several Albums or to none.
_Avoid_: Collection, folder, set

**Tag**:
A shared label that can be attached to any piece of content (Posts, Photos, Works) and connects them across sections.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, keyword

**Work**:
A real piece of work the Owner showcases, presented as a short overview plus a detailed write-up. Plural: Works.
_Avoid_: Project, portfolio item, case study

**Experiment**:
A small interactive piece (CSS, Three.js, shader) that runs live on the page in the Playground. It is built in code, unlike Works, which are written.
_Avoid_: Demo, toy, playground item

**Playground**:
The section of the site that collects Experiments.
_Avoid_: Lab, sandbox
